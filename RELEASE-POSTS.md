# Beeb 0.11.0 announcement

Beeb 0.11.0 is out: profiles with their own Continue Watching and Saved lists, a kids mode limited to CBeebies and CBBC, and optional household sync between Apple TVs.

Live is now Channels, with a full TV guide that goes up to eight hours back, plus Watch from start for live TV. You can choose what the Top Shelf shows, search no longer stalls while typing, and programme pages put the newest series first. There's also a first preview of Beeb for Mac.

Thanks to everyone who contributed pull requests to this release.

Download: https://github.com/usefulappmaker/Beeb-public/releases/tag/v0.11.0

The IPA is unsigned and needs your own signing before installation. Beeb is an independent, open-source BBC player for Apple TV.

# Beeb 0.10.9 hotfix announcement

Beeb's latest hotfix makes optional 4K setup much simpler: open Settings → Enable BBC UHD and connect directly on Apple TV. No computer helper, downloader, Python or copied setup link.

It also fixes Modern Topbar focus, adds a left-edge shortcut for the sidebar with return to the same item, makes Saved easier to reach from episode rows, and cleans up the Live page.

Download: https://github.com/usefulappmaker/Beeb-public/releases/tag/v0.10.9

The IPA is unsigned and needs your own signing before installation. Beeb is an independent, open-source BBC player for Apple TV.
